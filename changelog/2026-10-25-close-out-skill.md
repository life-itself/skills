A new draft [close-out](https://github.com/life-itself/skills/blob/main/skills/close-out/SKILL.md) skill makes "we're done" mean done: it first checks whether the session's goal is actually met (and says what is left if not), then sweeps the session for loose ends (asking only where it is unclear whether something was dropped), brings Beads, docs and the changelog up to date, commits and pushes, reports a short "closed out" summary, and marks the session ✅ in the Claude app.
