# Marketing pack: {campaign}

Ready-to-use copy, all cut from one core message. Adapt it, but don't change the facts. Strategy and audiences: [strategy.md](strategy.md).

## Key links

The primary CTA link first, with UTM tags if they're used.

## One-liner

One sentence: what it is, for whom, and the key fact (date, price).

## Short blurb (DM, WhatsApp, a short post)

60–100 words.

## Long blurb (LinkedIn, forum, forwarded email)

150–250 words.

## Per audience

For each segment in the strategy, the emphasis in one line, then a headline hook and 60–100 words.

## Hooks (indirect)

Start from the question or the concern, not the offer. Write one per audience.

## Social cards

Card copy, typically three: a hook, the details, proof (a quote or number).

## Press block

- Boilerplate at 25 and 100 words.
- Founder bio, short, with a headshot link.
- Two or three quotes and key facts.
- Logo and image links.
- A press contact email.
