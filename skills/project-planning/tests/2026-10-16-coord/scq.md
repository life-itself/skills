## Situation
- Since the week of 14 Sep there is a team rhythm: daily standups, Life Itself Coordination Function calls, and periodic Asdra+Fae+Rufus sessions.
- The intent is that Asdra and Fae keep planning moving while Rufus has limited availability; Rufus comes in for decisions and unblocking.
- Briefing and access are done (`lip-3p0`, closed 1 Oct); Asdra and Fae have assigned SCQ topics (`lip-kv8.*`).
- Team planning lives in the `life-itself/planning` repo: portfolio, Beads, the Sep–Dec roadmap, and a fortnight recap (first run 8 Oct).
  - Team workflow (12 Oct): project decisions go in the project's issue tree with answer, who and date.

## Complication
- The rhythm exists, but it does not give Rufus a picture he can see of what's moving, what's stuck and what needs him.
- Rufus does not know whether the meetings happen reliably, or whether decisions and follow-ups land in the planning repo.
- The SCQ topics are assigned, but Rufus has no current evidence of their progress and cannot tell which are stuck.
- Rufus feels it most: he is meant to decide and unblock but cannot see what needs him; Asdra and Fae may be left waiting on a decision.
- If nothing changes: a blocker gets missed or a decision is lost; work drifts and time goes on reconstructing what happened (Rufus's guess).

## Question
How should the Life Itself coordination rhythm work so that we can see what's moving, what's stuck, what needs a decision from Rufus, and what was decided?

Criteria:
- For: Rufus, Asdra and Fae.
- Asdra and Fae can keep work moving without waiting on Rufus by default.
- Decisions and follow-ups are easy to find afterwards.
- By when: working within about two weeks, i.e. by end of October (Rufus's guess).
- Effort: about 1–2 hours of Rufus's time a week, depending on decisions coming up (Rufus's guess).
- Off the table: a new heavy process; duplicating the trackers already in use (Beads, GitHub, Asana).
