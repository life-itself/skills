## Verdict

**Rewrite.** The question is usable, but the Situation mixes facts with interpretations, the complication has little concrete cost, and the tree needs clearer coverage.

## Rewritten SCQ

**Situation**

- The v2 site is live, with the Jack Smith report and about 18 reports in the archive.
- About-page copy and a launch audit landed on 10 Oct; the Bluesky poster is in dry-run.
- Traffic is about 3k uniques per month; what visitors come for is unknown.
- The 25 Sep plan named 13 Oct as a launch date; that date passed without a post.

**Complication**

- Site work has moved forward while public posting has stayed pending.
- Without posts, Rufus cannot learn which reports or excerpts draw a response.
- The 3 Nov midterms are 18 days away, shortening the remaining Jack Smith hook.
- The original one-report plan may not fit the larger archive.

**Question**

- How should Rufus launch RTM with the current archive to learn which reports or excerpts attract a response?

**Criteria**

- Target first public posts before 3 Nov 2026; feasibility is open.
- Audience: journalists, researchers, and politically engaged readers interested in these reports.
- Effort: a few focused hours of Rufus’s time.
- Success: posts are live and response can be compared across reports or excerpts.
- No commentary or opinion; no large build or additional reports before posting. Paid promotion remains undecided.

## Most useful skill change

Require the interviewer to ask **one neutral question per turn**, without offering examples or candidate solutions before the owner has answered.
