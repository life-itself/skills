## Where the skill was unclear or forced an awkward move

1. **No step for "is this the right project / right altitude?"** The 13 Sept research checkpoint says DAM is now a supporting capability under the comms content strategy and that there is no evidence asset-finding is the bottleneck. The sim confirmed it could not name one page or post that stalled. The flow still marches from complication to a DAM-scoped "how do we make media findable" question. I could only put the premise doubt in as tree branch 1 ("is finding media actually a real constraint?") and as the last complication bullet. That bullet undercuts the urgency (rubric C4), and the question presumes the problem (Q5). The skill needs a check after the complication, something like: "If the complication can't name a concrete cost, or the work sits under a bigger question, raise the question one level or make the premise the question."
2. **Criteria vs "prompt, don't draft".** Step 4 says a question without criteria isn't ready for sign-off. The sim had no date and no effort figure ("I can't give you a number"). Nothing tells the interviewer what to do then. I proposed "about a week" and marked it as my proposal. The sim accepted it with `[GUESS]`, and the sign-off came with the caveat "not agreed". Say whether the interviewer may propose a default, whether "unknown — owner to set" is an acceptable criterion, or whether this should block sign-off.
3. **"One question at a time" is broken by the flow itself.** Step 0 (explain) has to go out with step 2's first question, or it's a wasted turn. Step 4 has two asks ("is this the question?" and the criteria). Step 6 (show the path, say what was left out) and step 7 (the exact sign-off line) land in one message. I split step 4 into two turns and merged the others. Spell out which steps share a message.
4. **Choosing the output file is ambiguous when there is prior SCQ material.** The dam repo already has `MOTIVATION.md` (an SCQH), `docs/plans/2026-09-13-discovery-and-reuse-design.md`, and a de facto root question doc in another repo (`comms/docs/content-strategy.md`). "If a root question file exists, use its tree" and "resume, never overwrite" don't say whether an old SCQH counts as one to resume, or whether a parent root question doc in a sibling repo should be the target. I wrote a new file (in a real run, `dam/docs/plans/2026-10-16-dam-scq.md`).
5. **"Ideas offered" vs tree statuses.** The template allows `proposed` / `decided: <answer>` in the tree, while the principles say to park solutions. "Drive as archive" is both a proposed answer to a tree question and a solution. Rufus himself said "that's a proposed answer, not a question", so I moved it to the parked ideas and left the branch open. Clarify: when does an answer go in the tree as `proposed`, and when is it parked? Also, most parked ideas came from the notes, not from the user in session. The template says "solution the user floated", so I tagged each idea with where it came from.
6. **Playback: "is that right?" vs moving things.** In the situation playback I moved one clause to the complication and said so. That worked. But the step-2 rule ("move anything arguable to the complication") and "keep their wording" pull against each other a bit. A one-line "say what you moved" instruction would help.
7. **Step 8 item 3 is redundant.** The template already has the "Next" line. I extended it with "return to turn the result into beads". The template should carry that wording.
8. **The tree size limit was hit.** The first playback had 8 branches (limit 3–7). The user's additions pushed it up again, and I had to merge metadata with "what's strong". That was fine, but it's my editorial call, made after the last playback the user saw in full. The sign-off covered it only through the file.
